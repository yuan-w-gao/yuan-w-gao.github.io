Hi! I'm a 4th year PhD student at the <a href='https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/'> Department of Computer Science and Technology</a> at the <a href='https://www.cam.ac.uk/'>University of Cambridge</a> where I'm supervised by <a href='https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~ws390/index.html'>Dr. Weiwei Sun</a>. I am part of the <a href='http://www.wiki.cl.cam.ac.uk/clwiki/NaturalLanguage/ALTA'>ALTA Institute</a> (Automated Language Teaching and Assessment) directed by <a href='https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~pjb48/'>Prof Paula Buttery</a>. My research focuses on structured probabilistic modeling for graph-conditioned language generation, including latent-variable learning and inference, with applications to language acquisition and general graph settings.

I previously completed an <a href='https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/admissions/acs'>MPhil in Advanced Computer Science</a> at the University of Cambridge (with Distinction) supervised by <a href='https://andreasvlachos.github.io/'>Prof Andreas Vlachos</a>, where my thesis studied knowledge-grounded dialogue generation on Wikipedia. I received my BSc in Computer Science from <a href='https://www.cs.cmu.edu/'>the School of Computer Science</a> at <a href='https://www.cmu.edu/'>Carnegie Mellon University</a> with a minor in Math and graduated with University Honors.
